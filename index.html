01 / CONTEXT & REALITY

The Challenge Families Face in Under-Resourced Communities

Families of children with autism, cerebral palsy, and developmental delays in Nairobi face three severe barriers:

01.1
KES 4,500/hr

Cost & Exclusion

Private sessions cost KES 3,000–8,000/hr. For informal-wage families, continuous care is impossible without localized, subsidized therapy.

01.2
2.5+ hrs

Distance & Isolation

Crossing Nairobi in crowded matatus with a child experiencing sensory overload is exhausting. Therapy must be situated right where families live.

01.3
0 support

Stigma & Burnout

Caregivers face deep stigma and emotional isolation. MindBridge builds peer support circles so parents never walk this journey alone.

LEGAL & SAFEGUARDING FRAMEWORK

Privacy & Child Protection Policy

Governed by Stand Out 4 Mental Health (SO4MH) under the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019) and Children Act (2022).

1. Purpose & Scope

This policy outlines how Stand Out 4 Mental Health protects the rights, dignity, and personal data of children, caregivers, donors, and partners participating in the MindBridge Therapy & Rehabilitation Centre initiative in Nairobi.

2. Child Protection & Safe Spaces

MindBridge upholds zero tolerance for any form of physical, psychological, emotional abuse, or neglect. All licensed occupational therapists, speech therapists, clinical volunteers, and staff undergo stringent safeguarding vetting before interacting with children. Therapy rooms maintain open visibility and adhere to international pediatric safeguarding protocols.

3. Photography, Media & Storytelling Safeguards

Every photo, video, or quote featured in our campaigns is published exclusively with informed, written parental/guardian consent. We never publish full names of minors or exact home locations without explicit protective authorization.

4. Donor Data Privacy

When you pledge equipment or contribute via M-PESA Paybill (891300 / 148469), your contact details (phone, email, transaction code) are stored in encrypted form strictly for contribution verification, financial audits, and campaign updates. We never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose donor data to third parties.

5. Data Rights & Safeguarding Contact

You have the right to inspect, update, or request the deletion of your contact data from our active champion registry at any time. For child safeguarding concerns or privacy inquiries, contact our Safeguarding Desk:

  • Email: info@standout4mentalhealth.org
  • Helpline: +254 780 796 146
  • Location: Embakasi East & Mukuru Settlements, Nairobi, Kenya
FIDUCIARY INTEGRITY

Donor Transparency Charter

Our binding commitment to financial accountability, radical transparency, and community stewardship.

1. 100% Direct Equipping Guarantee

All contributions raised through the #100for100 campaign (M-PESA Paybill 891300, Account 148469) are exclusively ring-fenced for capital outfitting: high-density therapy mats, sensory swings, positioning cushions, tactile sensory panels, and AAC visual speech binders.

2. Public Itemized Equipping Ledger

At the conclusion of the KES 500,000 Equipping Phase, an itemized public financial report detailing vendor invoices, procurement records, and photographic proof of installed apparatus in the centre will be distributed to every registered Champion and made publicly accessible.

3. Community Governance & Oversight

Funds and procurement decisions are monitored by the Stand Out 4 Mental Health governance council in partnership with community representatives and allied pediatric clinicians from Embakasi East and Mukuru Kwa Njenga.

4. Site Visits & Verification Rights

All Champions and corporate allies are entitled to request an in-person walkthrough of the MindBridge facility to inspect secured equipment and observe caregiver sessions in practice. To schedule a visit:

  • Email: info@standout4mentalhealth.org
  • Coordination Phone: +254 780 796 146
